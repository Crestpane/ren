FAQ

Questions, answered.

All questions
How long does a website take?

It depends on the package and scope. We agree a timeline with you in the written quote before work starts. A simple one-page site can launch in as little as a week.

How much does a website cost?

Packages start at $249 under the current launch offer (50% off the standard $500). The pricing page lists every package and add-on, and each project gets a written quote.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Every site is built mobile-first and checked across phone, tablet and desktop widths before launch.

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes. Your domain and hosting are registered in your name, and after final payment you receive the source files and full rights to use them.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Send us the link and tell us what is not working, and we will propose changes and a fixed price.

Let’s build something exceptional.

Have a project in mind? Tell us about it and we’ll reply with questions, a fixed price and a timeline.
