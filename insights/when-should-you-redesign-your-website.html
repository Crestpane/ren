When Should You Redesign Your Website?

On this pageSigns it is timeSigns a smaller fix will doHow to approach it

Signs that a refresh is worth the effort, and signs it is not.

Signs it is time

The site looks dated next to competitors, is hard to use on phones, loads slowly or no longer matches what you offer.

Signs a smaller fix will do

If the structure and message are sound, updated content, new images and a speed pass may be enough.

How to approach it

Review what works, keep valuable page addresses, plan redirects and set a clear goal for the new site before design begins.
