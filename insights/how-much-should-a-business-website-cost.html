How Much Should a Business Website Cost?

On this pageWhat affects the priceCompare scope, not just totalsRemember the running costsOur own pricing

What drives the price of a website, and how to compare quotes fairly.

What affects the price

The main drivers are the number of pages, how much is custom design versus a template, the features needed (forms, bookings, payments) and how much content you supply yourself.

Compare scope, not just totals

Two quotes with different totals may include different things. Ask how many pages, how many revision rounds, what integrations and what happens after launch.

Remember the running costs

Domain registration, hosting and third-party services are usually separate from design fees. A good quote lists them so there are no surprises.
